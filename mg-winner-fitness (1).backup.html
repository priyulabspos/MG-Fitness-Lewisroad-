FAQ

Quick answers.

sample answers, gym to confirm
How do I join?

Tap Join MG Winner Fitness, send the enquiry or walk in. The team will help you pick a plan.

Can I visit before I join?

Yes. Send an enquiry and we will arrange a visit. Trial terms are set by the gym.

What are the opening hours?

Sample hours are in the Visit us section. The gym will confirm the real timings.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes, one-to-one coaching is one of the programs. Ask about it when you enquire.

Can I pause my membership?

Pause rules depend on the plan and are decided by the gym. Ask at the front desk.
